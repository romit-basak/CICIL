Juntúul miis ku janal tiʼ junpʼéel u kúuchil janal chúukaʼan yéetel u jejeláas jeetsʼel kʼaax yéetel chak kʼaax, maʼalob u bin táan u yuumil ichil junpʼéel yucateco maya jool naj.
Le ts'íib "T'aan ju'unil u k'a'ajsa'al u t'aanil Maya Yucateco" tu much'k'ab yéetel u ju'unil u k'a'ajsa'al máak ku ye'esik junp'éel much'ikil yéetel u yóolil u t'aanil maaya yucateco, ku ts'áaik nojbe'enil ti' u t'aanil yéetel u ka'ansajil.
Óoxpʼéel máak táan u meyajoʼob yéetel jumpʼéel computadora, wa maʼ u jatskubaʼob tiʼ jumpʼéel chan kúuchil u grabarku yéetel u xookil u tsʼíib xook.
Juntúul ts'uul café box yóok'ol junp'éel che' ma' tun chíikbesik mix ba'al u t'aan mayaa.
Teʼ luʼumil Maya, ku yilaʼal kʼoojil yakanil kaab tʼooxol, wa jach tumen u j-kanan kaab, teʼ kʼáax tuʼux yaan cheʼoʼ.
Juntúul chak p'íichil ja' plástico yéetel u ts'u'uk buluk' u yóok'ol ti' junp'éel k'áanche' chak'an yéetel u bixil kaj, chéen u meyaj.
U kúuchil juun múuchʼtáanil wa xooknajil yéetel meesajiloʼob, síiyajiloʼob yéetel baʼaloʼob jeʼex nuʼukuloʼob tiʼ jach kʼaʼanaʼan kʼaayiloʼob, maʼalob u beel yéetel u yóol máaya yucateka, kex mix junpʼéel baʼal ku yeʼesik baʼaloʼob úuchbenoʼob wa jatsʼuts beetaʼanoʼ
Ti junp’éel lu’umil kaajil maya yucateco, junp’éel xa’an yéetel junp’éel che’ yéetel sak-nikte’ k’áatnaj yiknal junp’éel pak’ k’íich, u ye’esik ba’ax ku beeta’al yéetel le loolo’ob yaan te’ lu’umo’.
Juntúul chan áak'al k'áax ku yantal te' najil tu'ux ku kajtal máak, ma'alob xan tu yóok'ol junp'éel u kúuchil pak'i.
Teʼ luʼumil trópico tuʼux yaan xaan yetel junpʼéel kaʼan sak, jumpʼéel kʼab ku yichikʼtal jumpʼéel lool chak, maʼalob u yeʼesik baʼalob yucatec maya.
Juntúul luʼumil kúuchil yaʼaxcheʼob yéetel nojoch pakalmoʼob, yéetel junpʼéel kaʼan sáasil, maʼalob u tsʼaʼabal óol yéetel u kʼaabaʼ máaya yucateka.
Juntúul ts'íib óolche' yéetel jump'éel maayail táan ichil, k'a'ana'an yéetel ya'ax che'o'ob yéetel jump'éel bej ts'a'ayal, ma'alob ka'ach jump'éel kúuchil nojil k'ajóoltbil yéetel le maayail kuxa'an oora.
Juntúul si’ipche’e’ ya’ax xíiwil yéetel chak lool, ma’alob u beel junp’éel chak lool, te’ tu kaajil yéetel junp’éel k’óox k’an kab.
Juntúul naj kʼan Acanceh, Yucatán, México, yéetel junpʼéel corredor yéetel junpʼéel kankab sak, maʼalob u beel u yeʼesik baʼaxoʼob u kʼaʼam óolal u talamil mayaj.
Juntúul pak'al ya'ax ki'ichkelem lool yéetel sak loolo'ob, ma'alob junp'éel jejeláas pak'al ti' u lu'umil Maya Yucatán.
Juntúul pak'al chúumuk che' yéetel ya'ax paapayo'ob te' lu'umil k'áax, wa láayli' ti' le maaya yucateco'obo'.
U sak-lol morado yéetel u chúumuk chak, ti' múuch'kabil yéetel ya'ax le'ob, k'ajóol bey jump'éel tu'ux ku yantal che'ob ichil k'áax wa tu'ux k'áaxil.
U sak kax ku kaxtik janal te'ej lu'umil yéetel cha'anil tu'ux yaan sak lu'um yéetel tak'an xa'an.
Juntúul cheʼ táabcheʼ yéetel juntúul cheʼ kúuchil kúuchil kúuchil tiʼ yichil junpʼéel joonaj tsʼíibtaʼan yéetel nichim, teʼ kaajil yéetel baʼaloʼob kex tuʼux kʼaʼanaʼan kʼaʼanaʼan tiʼ junpʼéel kúuchil yóokʼol kaab wa táanxelil kaaj.
Ti' le mayaa yucateca kuxtal, jump'éel che' yéetel ka'ap'éel u mootso'ob ku much'ikuba'ob ti' jump'éel chéen juntúul je'el u k'áat u ya'al u much'ikuba wa u yantalil, ku ye'esik bix u adorarkil le naturaleza yéetel le oksaj óolal ti' u páajtalil sagrado.
U yotoch jumpʼéel maaya yucateka tuʼux ku yantal paaxil yéetel cheʼoʼob, tiʼ jumpʼéel kʼaax.
Juntúul ko'olel maaya yucatekae' táan u meyajil kool ichil junp'éel paak'al tu jool ya'axche'o'ob.
U yóok'ol naj junp'éel táankab naj ichil u kúuchil kaajil Maya Yucatán, yéetel junp'éel k'áak' ku p'áatal k'áak'nak yéetel u nu'ukulil chokoj janal, ma'alob xan ti' u lu'umil Yucatán, México.
Tuukulnajil maaya yucateka, ku ts'a'abal janal yóok'ol k'áak' che' ichil junp'éel naj lu'um, ku k'a'amal tak'an k'áak'o'ob yéetel u nu'ukulil janal.
Juntúul p'aatal tu'ux yaan ya'abach empanada, ma'alob xan u janalil, ti' yaan ichil juntúul otoch wa tu beel tu'ux ku ko'onol.
Tuukulil najil yucatec maya cheʼil yookʼol xanil, jun túul miis kúuchul luʼumil ku yeʼesik junpʼéel jeetsʼel yéetel kʼajlayil kuxtal.
U yantal jumpʼéel koʼolel ku beetik tamales kʼaaxil yéetel ximbalnal janal beey kʼaaxil mayaj najoʼ.
Juntúul kaajil táankab maaya yéetel junpʼéel kóol tuunich yéetel kʼaʼax, maʼalob u pakʼik tradicional ixiʼim.
Juntúul máak ku xíimbal yóokʼol jumpʼéel bej ichil kʼáax, maʼalob u bin teʼ tuʼux yaan u najil úuchben maaya yucatecoʼoboʼ.
Juntúul ya'ax ch'úup, sak yéetel boox u ts'íibal, ku líik'il ti' junp'éel kúuchil jach bey u beeta'al tumen Yuumtsil, ti' ka'anal k'áan yéetel che'o'ob, ma'alob xan u much'ikuba yéetel u meyajilo'ob le ko'olelo'ob maya'obo'.
Jun tulumil ixiʼim teʼ luʼumil kooloʼ, wa maʼ u jelaʼaneʼ tiʼ junpʼéel kúuchil kʼáaxil wa tiʼ junpʼéel kúuchil jach kʼáaxil, yéetel junpʼéel naj beetaʼan yéetel cheʼ yéetel u yookʼol u boox kʼáan tu tséel.
Juntúul nal tak'an te' k'áax, wa ichil u lu'umil yucatec maya.
U jool najil kʼáax yéetel cheʼob yéetel kʼaʼam cheʼob tu tsʼuʼ kʼáax, tu yóokʼol jumpʼéel sáasil kaʼan, maʼalob u sáasil kʼiin.
U yóok'ol jump'éel iglesia te' lu'umil Maya yucateco, ts'a'aban u k'a'aytajil yéetel u péepen o u bandera, ma'alob ka'ach ti' jump'éel k'áak'náab ichil u yúul k'uj wa jump'éel ba'al jats'uts.
Ti junp’éel kúuchil yucateco tu’ux kaja’an máak, ku yila’al bix u beeta’al janal yéetel nu’ukulo’ob de chak lu’um yéetel junp’éel kúuchil de pak’a’an lu’um je’el bix u beeta’al janalo’ob yéetel ba’axo’ob ku beeta’al ichil naj.
Ka'ap'éel k'ab beetik meyaj múuch' ti' junp'éel che', ma'alob junp'éel meyaj yucateco maya beey xookil wa beeta'an ba'al.
U k'iinil u kuxtal máak ichil junp'éel chan kaaj maaya, yéetel ka'ap'éel peek'o'ob ichil u yóok'olnaajil yéetel junp'éel máak kúuchil ti' u joolnaajil junp'éel kúuchil naj.
U téejil le kúuchilaʼ ku yeʼesik junpʼéel bej naaj tu kaajil yéetel junpʼéel pakʼ tuunich yéetel junpʼéel naj sak tu kʼab óolal u yúuchben bixil u kuxtal u maaya kaajil Yucatán.
Juntúul naj cheʼ ichil u kʼaʼam óolal mayáa, yéetel u jool naj káan, jeʼex u beetaʼal teʼ noojol xamanil México yéetel Guatemala, tiʼ yaʼab kʼáax.
Jun túul ch'íich' kaxbil ti' junp'éel che'il najil yáanal lu'um, ma'alob ka'ach u beeta'al k'aaba'ob wa kuxtalil u meyajil u yóok'ol kaab Maya Yucateco.
Teʼ jun kúuchil táanxel luʼumil yucateco maya, juntúul miis sak yéetel éekʼ ku pʼáatal tiʼ junpʼéel cheʼ, tiʼ kʼaax yéetel junpʼéel naj yéetel kʼóop chʼíichʼ yéetel cheʼoʼob.
Juntúul máak ti' le mayao' táan u yéeyik bu'ul ich junp'éel k'áab, wa ma' xa'an ich junp'éel kúuchil naaj.
Juntúul mis tsíimin ku yantal tu yóokʼol junpʼéel kóom tuunich úuchben tu luʼumil, wa tu luʼumil maaya yucateco.
Uchben ts'uul ch'iwo'och kúuchil najil yéetel paak'al tu táan, k'a'ana'an yóok'ol kaab yucateco.
Jun tzeel cheʼeʼ yaan u jelaʼanil u beel yéetel u kʼaboʼob chʼikilil, kʼaʼanaʼan u kʼajóoltbil u kuxtal wa u yóol, tu tsʼíikil junpʼéel naj tuʼux ku muchʼikuba u yóol le u kúuchil kʼajóoltbil yéetel le u kʼaabaʼoʼob.
Jun t'úul che' ya'ax le'ob tu ts'u'ul yéetel k'áax le'ob te' táanxel lu'um, ma'alob xan te' k'iin k'áak'náab wa k'eex k'iin, ichil u k'a'ajsajil u yóol maya yucateco.
U síijil sakpakal boox yéetel chak xiknal t'uchul ti' junp'éel che' k'aaxil, ma' táan u yila'al mix junp'éel ba'al yucateco maya.
Teʼ luʼumil yucateco maaya kaajaloʼ, kaʼatúul x-kaax, jumpʼéel sak yéetel uláakʼ éekʼ, táan u xíimbal jach náatsʼ tiʼ junpʼéel cheʼ tsʼoʼok u tʼúubul teʼ jatsʼuts kúuchil yéetel xíiwoʼob yéetel túunichoʼoboʼ.
Táan u ye'esik junp'éel paaxil ts'íib tu kaajil Yucatán junp'éel chan x-ch'úupal táan u meyaj yéetel junp'éel ba'al ku yantal te' lu'umilo' ichil junp'éel kaajil.
Teʼ kaajil yéetel tu kúuchil kúuchil, junpʼéel cheʼ yaʼax kʼáan looloʼob chak, kʼaabaʼ bougainvillea, tiʼ ku yantal ichil junpʼéel bej táan u baʼpachtik yaʼax kʼáax.
